The Melbourne Java & JVM Users Group is a free quarterly meetup for Java and JVM developers in Melbourne. We get together four times a year in Melbourne CBD for pizza and a talk.

## Who it is for

Developers, students and anyone interested in Java and the JVM. That includes people who write Java for a living, people who work in Kotlin, Scala or another language that runs on the JVM, and people who are just getting started and want to meet others who use it.

You do not need to be an expert to come along, and you do not need to bring anything.

## What happens at a meetup

Each meetup starts with pizza, and the talk follows. The talk is the reason to come; the pizza and the chat beforehand are the reason to get there early. It is a good place to ask questions, hear what other teams are doing and meet people who work with the same tools you do.

## How to join

Everything runs through our Meetup page. Join the group there to see the next meetup, RSVP and get a note when a new one is announced. Membership is free and so is every meetup.
