01What to expect

A talk, some pizza and a room full of people who work with the JVM

Each meetup is built around one talk on Java or something else that runs on the JVM. After the talk there is pizza, and time to chat with the speaker and the other people in the room.

The group is free to join and free to attend. You do not need to be an expert, and you do not need to be working in Java right now. Students and people who are simply curious about the JVM are as welcome as developers who use it every day.

02Next meetup

Dates, talks and venues are announced on Meetup

We meet four times a year in Melbourne CBD. The date, the venue and the talk for each meetup are posted on our Meetup page, and that is also where you RSVP. Joining the group there means you hear about each meetup as soon as it is announced.
