We meet four times a year in Melbourne CBD. Each meetup starts with pizza, then one talk on Java or the JVM.

## Upcoming meetups

Upcoming events are listed on our Meetup page. That is where you will find the date, the venue and the talk for the next meetup, and where you RSVP so we know how much pizza to order.

<p class="actions"><a class="btn btn-accent" href="https://www.meetup.com/melbourne-java-jvm-users-group/">See upcoming events on Meetup</a></p>

## What a meetup looks like

- Pizza to start.
- Time to chat with the speaker and everyone else in the room.
- A talk by someone who works with Java or the JVM.

Meetups are free and open to developers, students and anyone interested in Java and the JVM.

## Past meetups

Past meetups, and photos from them, are on our Meetup page as well.

## Give a talk

If you have something to share, from a tool your team uses to a lesson you learned the hard way, we would like to hear about it. Tell us through the [contact page]({=site.url('contact')}).
