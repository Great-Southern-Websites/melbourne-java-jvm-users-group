Our meetups are free to attend, and we would like to keep them that way. Support from companies that work with Java and the JVM is what makes that possible.

## How you can help

Sponsorship can be as simple as a room for an evening or the pizza for the night. If your company would like to host a meetup, cover the food, or help in another way, we would like to hear from you.

## Get in touch

Tell us what you have in mind through the [contact page]({=site.url('contact')}) and we will get back to you.
