Our meetups are for developers, students and anyone interested in Java and the JVM. We want every one of them to feel welcome, whoever they are and however much they know. This code of conduct applies to everyone at a meetup, including speakers, organisers and sponsors, and to the group's Meetup page and any other place the group talks online.

## Be respectful

Treat everyone at a meetup with respect. Listen to the speaker. Let other people finish. Be kind in questions and in discussion, and remember that someone in the room is always newer to this than you are.

## Keep it welcoming

Harassment of any kind is not acceptable. That includes offensive comments about gender, sexual orientation, disability, appearance, race, religion or age, unwelcome sexual attention, deliberate intimidation, following someone around, or disrupting a talk. If someone asks you to stop, stop.

## If something goes wrong

If you see or experience behaviour that breaks this code, tell an organiser at the meetup, or send us a message through the [contact page]({=site.url('contact')}). We will listen, take it seriously and keep what you tell us private.

Anyone asked to stop unacceptable behaviour is expected to do so at once. Organisers may ask anyone who breaks this code to leave the meetup, and may ask them not to return.

## Thank you

Most of this comes down to being considerate. Thank you for helping keep the group a good place to learn and to meet people.
